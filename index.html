01

About

I care about how computing systems actually work, not just how to use them.

That means spending time in the layers most people prefer to abstract away: instruction sets, memory hierarchies, bare-metal firmware and the hardware that runs it all.

I'm drawn to systems programming: software that talks directly to hardware, has real constraints, and where a wrong pointer means a crash rather than a stack trace. C, C++ and Rust feel more honest to me than most things that run above them.

I'm doing my M.Sc. at IST through a dual-degree program with Poli-USP in Brazil. I have also been awarded in several national mathematics and physics competitions.
